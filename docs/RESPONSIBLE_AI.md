# Responsible AI

- Every person and account in the demo is synthetic. The simulator is not a substitute for representative bank data.
- Age band is reporting-only and excluded from the model feature vector.
- The latest local model run reported legitimate interruption rates by age band in `results/model_report.json`; the 65+ rate is compared with twice the overall rate. Thresholds require review before any real use.
- A model score prompts a question, hold or analyst escalation; no account is automatically frozen.
- The model and messages must be evaluated with older adults and scam-support specialists before deployment. The model is not independently validated for production decisions.
