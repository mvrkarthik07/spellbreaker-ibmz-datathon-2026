**Project title:** `tokenmaxxers — IBM Z Datathon 2026 Project`

**Description (150-word target):** SpellBreaker is a real-time intervention prototype for self-effected scams. Traditional identity checks can pass because the genuine customer is authorizing the transfer. SpellBreaker combines transaction context, minimal device interaction signals and receiver-side risk to estimate whether the customer may be under pressure. It selects a scam-specific question or pause, while a dashboard gives fraud analysts the decision context. The victim-side training examples are synthetic because banks cannot share real manipulated-customer cases; receiver-side risk uses XGBoost MuleNet trained against all 5,078,345 rows in IBM AMLworld HI-Small on LinuxONE. On the receiver-account holdout, MuleNet reports 7.46× lift and 7.46% recall in the top 1%, and 4.01× lift and 20.06% recall in the top 5%. The prototype uses fictional Merlion Bank customers only. Granite case-note generation and validation are implemented as an optional mode, but evaluation accepted 0/50 notes; the deterministic template remains the default. This prototype does not claim Hyper Protect, Secure Execution or Telum NNPA use. Submitted for **SSF2026**, Real-Time AI for Critical Decisions, with AI Secured as a secondary track.
