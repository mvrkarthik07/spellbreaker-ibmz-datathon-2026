**Description (150-word target):** SpellBreaker is a real-time intervention prototype for self-effected scams. Traditional identity checks can pass because the genuine customer is authorizing the transfer. SpellBreaker combines transaction context, minimal device interaction signals and receiver-side risk to estimate whether the customer may be under pressure. It selects a scam-specific question or pause, while a dashboard gives fraud analysts the decision context. The victim-side training examples are synthetic because banks cannot share real manipulated-customer cases; receiver-side risk uses MuleNet scores from a 2-million-row subset of IBM AMLworld HI-Small, trained on LinuxONE. The demo uses fictional Merlion Bank customers only. The prototype is designed to run within the bank platform and does not call an external AI API. LinuxONE deployment, s390x training and benchmark evidence are included. The long-term production target includes IBM Z security capabilities, but this prototype does not claim Hyper Protect, Secure Execution or Telum NNPA use. Submitted for **SSF2026**, Real-Time AI for Critical Decisions, with AI Secured as a secondary track.
